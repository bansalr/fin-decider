### `relevance.gsib_activity` · G-SIB Activity (multi_label)

- Definition: Material Markets, Lending, or Treasury/ALM activity performed by a large universal bank.
- Includes: bank lending; market making; trading; securities financing; derivatives; underwriting; syndication; balance-sheet management
- Excludes: asset-price movement without banking activity; issuer news without banking activity; **central-bank policy decision without dealer activity**; **bank share-price move only**
- **Label:** bank or dealer lending, underwriting, or trading
- **Question:** Does the article describe a bank, dealer, or lender doing business, such as lending, underwriting, trading, market making, financing clients, or managing its own funding?
- **True:** A bank or dealer's lending, deal, trade, client financing, or funding activity is reported, or a bank business line's results or strategy are discussed.
- **False:** Only prices, economic data, policy, or non-bank company news, with no bank or dealer activity described.
